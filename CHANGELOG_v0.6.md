# v0.6 Delta Changelog — Core Hypothesis Experiment

## Why this exists

Recent play footage raised a structural risk: the game can become “multiplayer ZBrush” — players safely add/remove volume against a precise target, while dangerous tools and multiplayer coordination are optional.

v0.6 does not declare a new final design. It builds a controlled A/B test to determine whether the stronger core is:

- **A:** precise shared sculpting; or
- **B:** a living customer who perceives the crew while an unavoidable physical validation event approaches.

## Main B-variant changes

- Helicopter validation overlaps construction instead of switching early to passive observation.
- Mid-round helicopter flyby acts as a small physical stress test.
- Final descent begins before work ends; players may keep repairing while landing occurs.
- Customer attention is driven by visible/sudden audible events, with `? -> ! -> reaction` experiment telegraphs.
- Mirror blocking uses real occlusion by player bodies / a magazine prop.
- Sudden sound sources create directional attention; sustained sources habituate.
- Helicopter proximity increasingly attracts customer attention upward / toward the window.
- Any player can temporarily brace the customer, trading their own tool use for reduced movement.
- Variant B uses an ad-style “desired outcome” reference rather than a technical construction blueprint.
- Exact shape score may continue to exist as hidden telemetry, but is not the visible success target in B.
- Growth material is finite enough that a near-total reset is not free.
- No microphone requirement in phase 1.

## Explicitly paused from deeper v0.5 investment

Do not use this experiment as justification to spend heavily on:

- more sculpt precision modes;
- exact three-view tracing;
- large material-state matrices;
- more customers;
- more commissions;
- meta progression;
- personal awards / ego economy expansion.

Useful existing v0.5 ideas such as readable hair states and physical props may remain, but are not the test variable here.
