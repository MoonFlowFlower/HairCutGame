# v0.5 Product Intent — From Brown Clay to Hair Material

## Problem statement

The current prototype can already express many target shapes through two universal operations: subtract with the precision clipper and add with growth spray. If those two are safe, accurate and reversible, most other tools become comedy-only accessories.

The solution is **not** to make the core tools unpleasant or inaccurate. The solution is to expand the material/action grammar so different mistakes create genuinely different problems.

## Desired player thought process

When a region is wrong, the player should naturally consider multiple qualitatively different fixes:

- "Cut the excess?"
- "Grow the missing side?"
- "Push the high side down?"
- "Pull the low side up?"
- "Move material from somewhere else?"
- "Flatten the whole face in one pass?"
- "Glue/freeze this before it collapses?"
- "Puncture it?"
- "Char/erode the edge?"

The prototype succeeds when those choices arise from visible material/tool behavior rather than a tool-tip telling players which key to use.

## Core fantasy

The head is a **low-poly soft sculpture made of hair-like material**:
- solid enough to carve and build,
- elastic enough to wobble and react,
- plastic enough to keep intentional deformation,
- stateful enough to become wet, young, frozen, glued or burnt,
- readable enough that a player can understand those states visually.

Normal hair should feel closer to dense foam / soft rubber / sculptable hair-mass than to liquid jelly. Precision remains possible.

## Goal language

A target job can now specify a combination of:

1. **Shape** — silhouette, hole, platform, arch, cavity, pillar, etc.
2. **Material state** — selected zones may need to be charred, frozen, wet, glued, young/soft, etc.
3. **Function** — the built thing must actually hold, guide, cushion, support, launch, land, carry or contain a real prop.

Do not require all three categories on every job. Prefer one strong shape requirement plus one or two readable material/function twists.

## Example commissions

### Helicopter Helipad
- broad flat top,
- enough stiffness/stability,
- actual pre-staged RC helicopter must land and dwell.

### Charred Birthday Cake
- recognizable layered cake shape,
- outer rim visibly charred,
- candles/props are physically placed before validation.

### Rocket Launch Silo
- straight central passage,
- inner wall may require controlled char/heat treatment,
- actual rocket prop is inserted before launch validation.

### Bird Nest
- bowl/cavity shape,
- center should remain relatively soft/cushioning,
- three real eggs are placed by players before validation and must remain supported.

### Hair Bridge
- spans the required gap,
- supports and/or glued/anchored at useful locations,
- real toy vehicle is staged before validation and traverses the bridge.

## Non-goals for v0.5

- full strand simulation,
- anatomically realistic hair,
- full FEM/softbody simulation,
- dozens of material states,
- large content expansion,
- complicated salon workflow,
- making every job require every tool.
