# 31 — v0.6 Core Hypothesis Experiment

> **状态更新（2026-09-29）**：用户已选择 B 为唯一开发主线，A 已归档。以下 A/B 实验流程和判定阈值保留作历史记录，不再是后续 B 开发的前置门槛；不代表实验已经通过。B 的设计约束仍适用，当前启动、验收与文档优先级见 [42 — B 开发基线](42_B_DEVELOPMENT_BASELINE.md)。


## Question

The experiment must answer:

> Does the game become more socially alive and replayable when the main pressure comes from **an approaching real validation event and a living customer whose attention the team can manage**, instead of from matching a precise target shape?

This is the highest-value unknown. Do not obscure it with unrelated new content.

## North star for Variant B

> Four barbers are trying to complete a functional absurd job on a customer who can see, hear and react, while the thing that will test their work is physically getting closer.

Every important player action should sit somewhere between two pressures:

- **Go faster / take a shortcut before validation arrives.**
- **Do not alarm the customer so much that the work becomes harder.**

The customer is not an “instability source”. Treat the customer as:

> **an audience the crew can manage, misdirect, calm and exploit.**

## Desired 30-second climax

A successful B round should be capable of producing a sequence like:

- helicopter enters final descent;
- downwash lifts an unsecured edge;
- customer looks up toward the noise;
- one player braces the head;
- another freezes/glues the lifting edge;
- another trims the protruding section while landing gear is already descending;
- the landing gear compresses the hair while the team still works;
- success/failure is decided visibly by the physical result.

The important part is **simultaneity**. Do not pause player agency to show the test.

## Experiment discipline

This version is successful even if Variant B loses, provided the test is fair and instrumented well enough to decide.

Do not tune B to “win” after seeing results. Use `docs/36_V06_PRE_REGISTERED_DECISION_RULES.md`.
