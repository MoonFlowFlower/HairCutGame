# 01 — Product Brief

## What the game is

A first-person 1–4 player physical-comedy barber game where all players work on the **same customer**.

The customer asks for an absurd functional head construction, not a normal haircut. Players freely grab tools that range from normal clippers to industrial blowers, glue guns, growth chemicals, hedge trimmers, sniper rifles, and flamethrowers.

Everyone shares the objective and payout. The comedy comes from conflicting solutions, unstable materials, a nervous living customer, and tools whose physical side effects affect the whole room.

## What it is not

- not a realistic haircut simulator,
- not Overcooked with four fixed salon jobs,
- not four isolated minigames,
- not a deathmatch disguised as a barber game,
- not a purely random-event comedy generator.

## Desired 30-second story

The team is building a helicopter landing pad from hair. One player carefully hedge-trims the top. Another sees a low spot and sprays growth chemical. A third uses a blower to flatten the new growth. The customer panics from the noise and ducks. The hedge trimmer slices a corner off. A fourth player takes a sniper rifle to make a precise correction; the customer turns toward a noise, the shot misses and hits a teammate. The teammate falls into the blower, which sends the growth mist across the entire head. The helicopter can already be heard approaching. Everyone starts shouting and trying to repair the same disaster.

This causal escalation is the product.

## Design mantra

> **Same head. Different ideas. Everyone is helping. Everything is getting worse.**

## Content philosophy

New content should add new interactions, not only skins.

Good tool:
- has a simple physical rule,
- solves multiple tasks,
- can create collateral consequences,
- interacts with other tools/states.

Good customer:
- has one memorable behavior that changes play,
- reuses the core head-material system where possible,
- creates understandable risk rather than constant nuisance.

Good goal:
- can be explained visually in seconds,
- invites multiple methods,
- has a physical validation moment,
- can fail spectacularly in a way players understand.
